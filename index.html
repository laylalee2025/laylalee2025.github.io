About

Hello, I'm Taeyoung Lee.

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University, advised by Prof. Hyeoncheol Kim. My research focuses on AI in Education (AIED), Human-AI Interaction, and Knowledge Tracing.

I'm particularly interested in how learner models can represent what individual students know and how they learn, and how such models can inform the design of AI systems that support learners in classroom settings.
